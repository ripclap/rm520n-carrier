1. Actual drilling and covering
The current board contains 1,161 drilled sites: 1,124 vias, 24 solderable component PTHs, nine NPTHs, and four U10 EP49 thermal PTHs. The vias comprise 158 Type VII nonconductive filled holes with copper caps on both faces and 966 ordinary unfilled/uncapped continuous bilateral soldermask tents. The four U10 thermal PTHs separately require Type VII closed bilateral caps, 625 µm lands/300 µm nominal holes, their existing front EP49 solder interface, closed back mask and no back paste. KiCad pad metadata does not encode the thermal PTH fill/cap process. Do not treat component slots or NPTHs as ordinary vias, or resize/shift any site without a reviewed job revision.

2. Site-specific finished bores
The 81 nominal 150 µm vias are differentiated: 25 have 400 µm lands and requested finished bores 70–170 µm; 55 have 460 µm lands and 70–230 µm; one OE site has a 450 µm land and 70–220 µm. Retain the named ordinary 450/200 µm site profiles of finished 120–220 µm in the schedule. The sole new direct-V12 550/200 µm site 9ee37410-49af-5bbc-b978-d716eac22bde at (52.7,46.0) mm requests finished 120–280 µm. That bound does not apply to other nominal 200 µm sites.
For sites without a separately qualified interval, propose and record an exact compatible finished-bore, drilling, registration, etch and plating recipe. The public nominal ±80 µm values and the calculated 26 µm radial etch/35 µm registration corners are diagnostics, not an accepted blanket process or simultaneous supplier guarantee. Require actual finished annulus at least 50 µm on every copper layer, no breakout, and minimum LOCAL barrel plating wall 20 µm. Average wall thickness is insufficient. The electrical 18 µm sensitivity calculation is not the default fabrication requirement. Use representative microsections/coupons and complete electrical test. Preserve actual source conductors and return/reference planes when compensating artwork.

4. Actual mask, paste and underside interfaces
The four actual exported mask/paste layers are checked for all 1,124 vias. The 966 ordinary lands are nominally closed on both faces and paste-free. The 158 capped sites allow only their listed exact same-net owner-terminal apertures; there are no standalone via stencil windows or unowned apertures. Keep all existing solderable component apertures. Nominal Gerber closure is not proof of sealed covering after actual registration and reflow.
Type VII caps must be bilaterally closed, with qualified flatness/proudness at most 10 µm. Ordinary mask peaks and mask above caps must be at most 25 µm above the corresponding adjacent finished copper/cap. For a listed underbody cover, retain at least 10 µm actual clearance to the cover; an unknown/zero-standoff non-owner body requires measured underside clearance of at least 35 µm for ordinary mask or 45 µm for a 10 µm cap plus 25 µm mask. An actual solder-owner terminal instead requires its exact qualified flat-cap/solder-seal/stencil/seating contract, including the U4 pair above. Confirm actual front/back plastic, leads, seating and mask integrity. A body/courtyard projection is not itself a foreign-metal collision; mask or caps cannot waive foreign pad/copper/raw-bore gaps, annuli or source/reference conditions.

Covering does not remove this requirement. The U4 coupled pair has the separate explicit local recipe in section 3. Do not use the seven-row list as a census of every possible whole-board raw-tool diagnostic.
