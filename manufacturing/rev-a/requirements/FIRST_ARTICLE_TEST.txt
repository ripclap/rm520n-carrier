RM520N-GL carrier Rev A — first article commissioning

Purpose: qualify the first manufactured assembly before installing an expensive
modem or repeating assembly of a larger lot. These are acceptance measurements
to perform on hardware; the CAD and numerical reviews do not claim they were run.

1. Receiving inspection
Compare the fabrication/assembly revision and supplied MPNs with the release
manifest and fitted BOM. Review drill/annulus/wall/cap/cover inspection records
against the per-site fabrication schedule. Inspect both faces and X-ray the
exposed-pad and filled/capped terminal interfaces. Inspect the specified H1 SMT
spacer solder deposit and final seated card support height. Confirm the SIM card
can be inserted through the intended enclosure opening without removing the PCB.
Leave the RM520N-GL and antennas unfitted for initial power/fault testing.

2. Safe initial power and normal operation
Use a current-limited, regulated 12 V +/-5% source at J2 (pin 1 positive, pin 2
GND), or the specified regulated 48 V +/-5% passive injector for J1; first test
each input separately, with USB and the modem disconnected. Verify TP1/V12,
TP2/V3V7, TP3/V3V3 and TP4/V0V95 against the released rail limits. Check always-on
3.3 V and the independently switched radio rail. Record startup, source transfer,
input current and converter temperatures. Prove reverse blocking and check that
the external USB VBUS cannot power the modem or management rails unexpectedly.
For passive input, verify actual loaded FILTER at least 41.562266501 V and
unloaded startup at least 41.977840535 V. For auxiliary input, verify at least
11.4 V at the actual J2 pins and use rated 3 A wiring. Lower-voltage energy or
impedance diagnostics do not reduce these installation limits.

Connect an instrumented dummy load in place of the modem. Exercise the declared
modem load, including load steps and cold/hot startup at both input limits.
Qualify any 4 A load step after actual protected PG/output validity. Healthy
input-bank qualification around 407 ms, the 20.9096 ms minimum fault timer, and
the hardware PG/FCPO qualification of at least 180 ms are separate events.
The U3 startup-current screen of 5.23055 A is not an input-energy guarantee.
The forced 4 A/all-selected-capacitors energy quotient of 31.657406 ms exceeds
the minimum fault timer and is held; it is not an admitted startup rating or
a guaranteed startup deadline.
The continuous operating target is 3 A at 0–40 C ambient. Begin any sustained 4 A
static test at or below 20 C, subject to the final released numerical contract.
The current complete actual-board calculation supports this initial limit only
with the specified efficiency, finished metal and cooling conditions. Higher
ambient or longer peak operation requires measured efficiency, the actual
thermal path and junction temperature at most 125 C, as well as the normal input
RMS limit. A 4 A electrical voltage screen alone is not a continuous thermal
rating, and no peak duty cycle is assumed.
For the selected operating target, require a measured main-stage thermal path
of at most 32 C/W and junction temperature at most 125 C. Keep component and
winding case temperatures within the released 70 C engineering ceiling.
The RM520N-GL normal VCC window is 3.135–4.4 V;
require the measured waveform, including measurement uncertainty, to remain
inside that window and ripple to stay below 100 mV peak-to-peak. Numerical
review includes rail loss and separate engineering reserves; it does not admit
a 4.4 A continuous load. Record calibrated voltage and temperature at the modem
connector, using a short-ground or differential measurement arrangement.
Require measured hot eFuse resistance at most 50 mOhm and junction at most 125 C,
actual modem-buck efficiency at least 88%, main-converter efficiency at least 90%,
always-on efficiency at least 82%, and each of the two cascaded Ethernet
regulators at least 82% in their declared load cases. These are engineering
commissioning requirements, not guaranteed manufacturer efficiencies.
Verify the separate always-on 850 mA
allocation plus the 3.75 mA observer/bus allowance. For passive PoE, require
normal input RMS at most 0.80 A for the aggregate input AND conservative each
injection leg, including PWM/ripple and real load bursts. Do not infer equal current
sharing. Measure powered Ethernet behavior, winding DC imbalance and temperature
at the actual cable/CT conditions; keep magnetics case at most 70 C. A mean
input-current calculation alone does not pass this RMS gate. Record component and enclosure
temperatures and the cooling arrangement used for every load result.

3. Protection, recovery and independent management
With the modem still absent, use a qualified current-limited fault fixture to
test overvoltage cutoff, short circuit/overload, latch/rearm and input removal.
Test protected OUT shorts and direct REG shorts as separate cases, recording
regulator current, inductor current and hiccup/latched response rather than
assuming the protected OUT switch also limits a short ahead of that switch.
Capture the modem-rail peak, response time, and time above 4.4 V. Require peak
plus measurement uncertainty to remain below the 4.7 V absolute maximum and
post-threshold rise to remain at or below the 100 mV engineering allowance.
The absolute maximum does not permit continued normal operation above 4.4 V;
OVLO is recoverable, and its published response time is typical rather than a
guaranteed maximum. If either bound fails, revise the hardware before fitting
the modem. A firmware delay is not fast overvoltage protection.

Prove that modem OFF/reset preserves management MCU and LoRa power. Confirm
that BOTH permanent REG bleeds, R221 and R779, are fitted as 1 kOhm from
V3V7_REG to GND. Observe REG decay with joint EN low and verify protected OUT
discharge and the released backfeed/injection limits. The 5 s joint-EN dwell
is an operational firmware/host recovery procedure; blank/reset MCU hardware
autostart does not implement that dwell. Modem power recovery must hold BOTH
joint buck/eFuse EN low for at least 5 s AND verify actual
V3V7 below 100 mV before restart, restarting the dwell after controller reset.
Radio recovery must hold RUN low for at least 350 ms AND verify its rail below
100 mV. A healthy blank/reset MCU must allow POWER_OK-qualified hardware
modem autostart, while radio remains OFF and USB grant remains disabled.
An absent physical joint-EN hardware driver must leave joint EN fail-safe LOW;
that is a different condition from an installed blank/reset MCU. Verify these
defaults, hardware watchdog recovery, hardware power-good qualification, and
no backfeed through USB, UART service
pads, status buses or radio isolation. Total input loss removes management power;
this design has no battery ride-through.

4. Modem and data interfaces
After the preceding gates pass, fit the exact production RM520N-GL firmware and
record its revision. Verify startup, FULL_CARD_POWER_OFF and RESET timing against
that firmware's Quectel requirements; hold the manual FCPO request for at least
1 s. Verify the hardware FCPO release qualification delays at least 180 ms after
actual protected PG/output validity; this is separate from manual FCPO request
timing and the joint EN recovery dwell. Verify 2.5G Ethernet negotiation and sustained traffic at the selected
magnetics' temperature/current conditions, all four cable pairs, input limits,
DC imbalance and transmit bursts. Validate high-speed signal performance on the
manufactured stack/coupons; CAD impedance geometry is not an eye-diagram test.

Test USB-C service attach, unplug, both mux ownership selections, powered-off
states and granted power-good states. Verify external VBUS-valid and module PG
hardware isolation before exercising the internal ESP32-S3 USB full-speed host
with the intended Quectel AT endpoints. USB enumeration and AT support depend
on the exact module firmware. This board has no assumed M.2 debug UART; SSH or
console support over the planned LoRa OOB network must be verified as a software
service on that firmware, not inferred from the copper layout.

Test physical SIM insertion/removal/detect, modem SIM selection and the sourced
eUICC profile/provisioning. Verify management telemetry calibration and I2C
addresses (NCA9536 0x41; temperature sensor 0x48). Treat current telemetry as
coarse until calibrated over its specified range.

5. LoRa and enclosure
Configure the E22-900MM22S for the selected US 915 MHz network plan and verify
SPI/control timing, radio reset, fault recovery and link performance with the
actual antenna and enclosure. Verify RF operation and deployment compliance for
the final assembly/network configuration. Authenticate recovery commands, bound
command duration and retry count, reject replayed commands, and log resets.
The network architecture remains software work; no arbitrary shell transport or
production OOB network behavior is claimed by this first PCB revision.

Record a signed first-article result with supply/firmware/fixture revisions,
waveforms, temperatures, measurements and disposition for every failed gate.
