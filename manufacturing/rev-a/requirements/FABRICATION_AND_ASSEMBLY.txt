RM520N-GL carrier Rev A — fabrication and assembly order requirements

Use the complete release Gerber, drill, IPC-D-356, via/hole schedule, fitted BOM,
placement and assembly drawing set together. Revision and SHA-256 are recorded
in the release manifest. Requirements below are an engineering order contract;
they are not a claim that a particular supplier has accepted or manufactured it.
Request a job-specific CAM/assembly disposition for any capability mismatch.

Power review requires finished outer copper at least 30 um, In2 and In3 each
at least 15.2 um, and In1 ground copper at least 12 um for the declared profile.
Reduced In2 or In3 copper of 12 um/local via wall18 um was a separately calculated sensitivity case, not the
default job. Require minimum local finished via barrel copper20 um. Do not
substitute a plating average for this local minimum. Record measured finished
wall, annulus and actual bore ranges on coupons/microsections.

Preserve the reference plane geometry, copper clearances and copper keepouts.
Front high-speed traces reference In1; back traces reference In4. Controlled
impedance groups include USB 90 ohm differential, PCIe/REFCLK 85 ohm differential,
MDI/cable 100 ohm differential and LoRa 50 ohm. Main drawn outer geometries are
USB width 0.136/gap 0.150 mm, PCIe/REFCLK 0.154/0.150 and MDI 0.104/0.150. Cable inner
and package launches use their exact reviewed geometry. Use impedance coupons
and a job-specific stack/etch calculation, with nominal target tolerance 10%.
Any proposed stack, material or artwork compensation affecting spacing, power
electrodes, reference continuity or launch geometry requires engineering review.

The per-site schedule is authoritative for drill/land/cover requirements. There
are 1124 through vias: 158 filled/capped Type VII and 966 ordinary; all physical
holes, including mechanical holes and four U10 exposed-pad thermal PTHs, are
listed separately, for 1161 physical holes. There are 81 nominal 150 um via drills.
Nominal 150 um drill sites have differentiated finished-bore
upper bounds depending on actual land geometry; do not apply 230 um to every
site. Require no breakout and measured finished copper annulus at least 50 um
on all six copper layers. Drill-position, etch and plating processes must jointly meet
the site/source/body/clearance requirements, not only the nominal drill list.

Filled/capped sites require void-controlled nonconductive fill and planar copper
caps on both faces. Maximum cap protrusion 10 um. Preserve each named terminal
mask/paste aperture; allow no extra via paste opening. Ordinary sites require
continuous, qualified bilateral solder-mask cover with the actual finished bore
and mask registration. A partial same-net PAD-mask overlap is not a claim that
the whole via land is masked. Maximum local ordinary cover peak 25 um. Under
parts, measure actual underside clearance above cover/caps; the individual
35/45 um clearance gates and tighter terminal conditions remain in the schedule.
Seven retained special doubled-growth diagnostic rows require explicit CAM
review. The 155 current possible large-bore Type VI fallback overrides are inactive. Any
needed alternative requires a specific per-site engineering disposition; do not
silently change cover type or enlarge drills.

The two U4 VIN vias have a coupled drill-position/raw-web recipe in
U4_VIN_FACTORY_COUPLED_REQUIREMENTS1190.txt. Both remain filled/capped Type VII.
The 450 um land at60.20,72.85 mm uses nominal 200 um drill and requested finished
120–220 um; the400 um land at60.70,72.75 mm uses nominal 150 um drill and
requested finished 70–170 um. The engineering witness requires maximum raw
diameters 250/200 um respectively, local wall at least 20 um, and each drill
position within 30 um in both axes. That combined witness leaves 216.815 um
raw web against the 206 um requirement. Verify all actual six-layer annuli,
source contacts and cap/mask/paste interfaces. Supplier process acceptance is
required; this witness is not a claim that a generic drill tolerance passes.

Assemble all purchased fitted BOM items with exact MPNs. No substitutions without
electrical, footprint, height and lifecycle review. The four fitted through-hole
items C725, J1, R727, J2 need the separate manual/selective-solder operation; they
are not missing SMT placements. Board test/service pads and mechanical-only
features are not purchased components. H1 IS a fitted SMT Würth 9774025243R spacer
and must be included. J800 IS an unpurchased service-pad pattern.

H1 requires the primary local stencil OD 5.2/ID 3.1 mm segmented annulus with 0.6 mm
gaps and at least 150 um paste deposit; use a qualified local step/paste operation
without applying 150 um deposit to unrelated fine-pitch ICs. Inspect final spacer
height/card seating and use the separately specified M2x3 retention screw with
head/washer/protrusion checked; primary maximum torque 0.2 Nm, lower if actual
PCB/module hardware requires it. Keep parts beneath the module within the
released body/height limits and verify the actual production card/enclosure fit.

E22-900MM22S sourcing in the cost model requires the exact JLCPCB Standard PCBA
allocation; there is no assumed standalone offer for another builder. Confirm
all quoted order quantities, pack handling and supply dates before procurement.
The RM520N-GL, antennas, cables, retention hardware outside the fitted BOM,
enclosure and eUICC provisioning are separate supplied items.

Installation contract: regulated 48 V +/-5% passive nonisolated SELV PoE, or
regulated 12 V +/-5% at J2, dry ventilated 0–40 C ambient at at most 2000 m altitude.
J2 pin 1 positive/pin 2 GND; a correctly wired rated barrel-to-JST adapter may be
used. This passive-input revision does not negotiate IEEE PoE/PoE+/PoE++ power.
Use the designated injector/adapter and verify polarity/voltage before connection.
The separately saved PoE++ design is not the fabrication input for this package.

Complete receiving inspection and dummy-load normal/fault/thermal/backfeed gates
in FIRST_ARTICLE_TEST.txt before fitting the modem. After those pass, fit the
modem and complete USB/Ethernet/SIM/LoRa and enclosure tests before repeating a
larger lot. Preserve test records with the exact hardware/firmware revision.
