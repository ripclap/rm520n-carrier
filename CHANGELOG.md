# Changelog

## Rev A — 2026-10-12

Initial public first-prototype release.

- Published native KiCad 10 source for a 110 × 96 mm, six-layer RM520N-GL carrier.
- Added RTL8125BG 2.5 GbE, front-face RJ45, edge-accessible physical SIM, and experimental soldered eUICC population.
- Included regulated 48 V passive PoE and 12 V auxiliary input, protected modem power, and independently powered ESP32-S3/LoRa management hardware.
- Published Gerbers, drills, IPC-D-356 netlist, fitted BOM, placement files, assembly drawings, and per-site fabrication requirements.
- Included front/back assembly renders and fabrication and first-article procedures.

Supplier CAM acceptance and physical qualification are pending. Management firmware and the remote LoRa network are not part of this release. See [validation status](docs/validation.md).
